Compare teen treatment transport quotes in Dallas–Fort Worth

A quote is easier to compare when every provider is pricing the same trip. Use this guide to describe a planned ride to treatment from North Texas, find out who travels with your teen, and get the total cost and terms in writing.

Where to start

Give each provider the same pickup and destination, intake window, proposed travel date, and support needs. Ask for a door-to-door price, the specific services included, and the charges that can change if the itinerary changes.

Send the same trip brief to every provider

For the first call, have the pickup city or ZIP code, the receiving program’s city, and its target intake window. Say whether the pickup is at home, a hospital, or another care setting. Share private clinical details only through a channel the care team or provider identifies for that purpose.

Ask the treating team what support is appropriate for the entire route. Discuss what would make a ride with a caregiver workable, what a private escort could provide, and whether the situation calls for a different level of transport. Invite your teen to describe travel needs and preferences.

  • Staffing and scopeWho will actually be on the trip? Ask for the number of staff, qualifications, roles, the name of any subcontractor, and how someone with decision-making authority can be reached during travel.
  • Written termsAsk which costs are fixed or estimated, who pays for tickets and last-mile rides, and what delay, overnight, rescheduling, and cancellation terms apply.
  • Family communicationAsk how your teen can reach a trusted adult, how the family will get updates, and how the provider will handle distress or an unsafe change in circumstances.

Compare the whole route, including time on the ground

For ground travel, compare the number of hours door to door, planned breaks, accessible vehicle needs, and where staff coverage may change. For a flight, count the pickup drive, check-in and screening time, layover if any, and the ride from the arrival airport to admissions.

DFW Airport publishes guidance on wheelchair service through the airline, its voluntary Hidden Disabilities Sunflower program, and TSA Cares screening support. DFW recommends requesting TSA Cares assistance at least 72 hours before departure. Contact the airline about its own seating and airport assistance services. [1] [2]

  • Travel dayRecord the actual airport and terminal, operating airline, pickup location, staff contact, and local time at the destination. A date without an intake cutoff can hide an overnight gap.
  • Delay planAsk who contacts admissions if travel runs late and who is responsible for your teen if the flight cancels or admissions closes.
  • Door-to-door totalCompare transport fees plus separately billed airfare, mileage, parking, staff travel, lodging, waiting and overtime. Ask for an example of when the bill would exceed the quote.

Know who to call in North Texas if care needs change

If the intake date moves, check with the treating team before changing the travel plan. For local referrals, Texas Health and Human Services lists the local mental or behavioral health authority by county. The agency differs across Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, and Denton counties. [3]

Call or text 988 for crisis support. For an immediate threat to life or a medical emergency, call 911. If private travel is already planned, coordinate any change with the care team and receiving program. [4]

  • Dallas and Tarrant countiesTexas HHS lists North Texas Behavioral Health Authority for Dallas County and MHMR of Tarrant County for Tarrant County. [3]
  • Collin and Denton countiesTexas HHS lists LifePath Systems for Collin County and Denton County MHMR Center for Denton County. [3]

Common questions

How much does teen treatment transport cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

An itemized quote for the actual trip is the best comparison. Give each provider the same route, timing, and support needs, then compare staffing, mileage or tickets, waiting, and change fees.

Can my teen fly from DFW to an out-of-state program?

Ask the treating team whether commercial air travel fits the teen’s current needs. Confirm the airline’s requirements, support arrangements, destination ground ride, and intake cutoff before booking.

What can I ask for at DFW Airport if my teen needs additional help?

Check DFW’s accessibility guidance for the voluntary Sunflower program and TSA Cares. For airline-provided assistance, speak directly with the carrier. [1] [2]

What if insurance might pay for the trip?

Call the insurer with the travel plan and itemized estimate. Ask for a written coverage decision, whether preauthorization is needed, and which charges will be your responsibility.
